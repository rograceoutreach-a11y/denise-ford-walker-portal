A Message from the Minister

Welcome home, beloved.

Songwriter · Author · Minister of Worship

I am honored God has gifted me with the ministry of songwriting, lyrical worship, and inspirational writing. Through 8 studio albums, published books, and the Praying to Know Him curriculum, my prayer is that each melody and every page would draw you closer to the heart of the Father.

Reflections of Grace Outreach Ministries is a ministry without walls — meeting you where you are, inspiring souls, and removing the mask through the Word of God.
